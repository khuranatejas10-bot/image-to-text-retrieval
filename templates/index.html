OCR & Fuzzy Matching Engine

A lightweight and robust image search system based on Devishree Naidu et al. It applies geometric deskewing, line removal, and adaptive thresholding prior to deep OCR extraction, indexing matches in SQLite using Levenshtein fuzzy matching.
